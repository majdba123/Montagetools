HEXA Video Builder V31.0.3 - Density Story Choreography

Engine: HEXA_VIDEO_BUILDER_V31_0_3_DENSITY_STORY_CHOREOGRAPHY

Root failure fixed:
- Scene-local semantic IDs were compared globally inside merged visual cards, collapsing
  distinct source objects into one identity.
- Phase planning serialized valid objects and treated atomic composites as exclusive rather
  than merely indivisible.
- The layout objective rewarded small footprints and penalized the occupancy range required
  for an intentional editorial composition.
- Disappearance opacity reached zero before phase boundaries, creating blank handoff troughs.

Generalized changes:
- Semantic identities and explicit relationship endpoints are scoped by source scene.
- Grammar-aware, simultaneous-first phases retain valid objects and use bounded waves when
  the two-primary concurrency rule requires temporal progression.
- Atomic assets remain intact but may coexist when the exact motion envelopes are safe.
- Density-aware scale/layout search targets meaningful safe-frame occupation and keeps both
  density-optimal and compact recovery branches.
- Disappearance zero-opacity is aligned to the phase boundary; singleton cards start with a
  safe anchored appearance instead of an offscreen blank lead-in.
- Visual-density and temporal-population diagnostics are emitted before rendering and become
  QA authority for multi-object serialization, near-blank duration, occupancy, balance and
  visual-island count.
- Continuous render/cache namespaces are invalidated for 31.0.3.

- Active objects: 35/70 -> 63/70

- Plan QA: PASS, zero failures, zero warnings
